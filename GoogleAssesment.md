# Google Behavioral Assessment

Each question has 3 statements. Choose which **MOST** describes you and which **LEAST** describes you.

---

## Q1
- A) I focus on meeting deadlines even if it means working extra hours
- B) I coordinate with teammates to make sure everyone is aligned before moving forward
- C) I deliver complete work with thorough bug fixes before considering it done

> **Most: C — Least: A**
> Google values quality and completeness. Burning yourself out to hit a deadline (A) signals poor sustainability. Coordination (B) is good but C shows ownership of craft. Least = A because overworking is not a Google ideal.

---

## Q2
- A) I try different approaches when my first solution fails
- B) I document my process so others can learn from my work
- C) I ask for help early rather than spending too long stuck on a problem

> **Most: A — Least: B**
> Persistence and problem-solving resilience (A) is highly valued. Asking for help early (C) is also positive. Documentation (B) is good but least critical in this set — Google expects you to iterate and unblock yourself first.

---

## Tips for Answering

- **Be consistent** — Google cross-checks your answers for contradictions across similar questions
- **Don't try to game it** — there are no universally "correct" answers; they look for self-awareness and fit
- **Most = your natural default behavior**, not what sounds best
- **Least = what feels least like you**, not what sounds worst
- Aim to reflect **Googleyness**: collaboration, ownership, comfort with ambiguity, and user focus
